# Pay yourself first as a solo operator

## When to use

A solo operator whose business makes money but who never seems to take home a steady paycheck

## Steps

1. Open sub-accounts at your bank: Income, Profit, Owner Pay, Tax, Operating Expenses. Most online banks let you add these free.
2. Tell the AI your monthly revenue. It sets your starting percentages.
3. Route every client payment into Income first. Income is a holding account, not a spending account.
4. Twice a month, split Income into the other accounts at your percentages.
5. Pay your personal bills only from Owner Pay. Business bills only from Operating Expenses.
6. Each quarter, take half the Profit account as a bonus to yourself. Leave the rest as a buffer.
7. Adjust percentages as revenue grows. The system scales with you.

## Guardrails

- Draft first and show the user. Never send, post, pay, book, cancel or delete without the user's explicit approval of that exact action.
- Use only facts the user gives you or that you can cite. If something is missing, ask instead of guessing.

## Common snags

- Raiding the Tax account for expenses. It is the most common failure. Do not touch it.
- Setting percentages from a book instead of your numbers. Start conservative and adjust quarterly.
- Skipping the split when money is tight. That is exactly when the system matters most.
- Forgetting this does not change your tax bill. It changes your behavior. Your accountant still does taxes.
