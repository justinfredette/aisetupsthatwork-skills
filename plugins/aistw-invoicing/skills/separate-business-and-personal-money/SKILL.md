# Separate your business and personal money for good

## When to use

A freelancer or side-gig worker running everything through one personal checking account

## Steps

1. List your recurring business expenses from the last 3 months of statements.
2. Paste the prompt below. The AI gives you a separation plan in order.
3. Open a separate business checking account. Many are free and take 20 minutes online.
4. Get a dedicated card for business spending. One card, business only.
5. Move your recurring business charges to the new account and card.
6. Sort the last 3 months of mixed transactions with the AI's help: business or personal.
7. Set one weekly habit: 10 minutes every Friday moving receipts and checking the split.

## Prompt

Fill the [BRACKETED] parts with the user, then follow this prompt:

```
Help me separate my business and personal finances. I am a [freelancer / sole proprietor / side-gig worker]. Right now everything runs through one personal account. My recurring business expenses are: [LIST]. Give me a step-by-step separation plan: 1) what accounts to open, 2) what to move first, 3) how to sort the last 3 months of mixed transactions, 4) one weekly habit to keep them separate. Keep it simple enough to finish in a weekend.
```

## Guardrails

- Draft first and show the user. Never send, post, pay, book, cancel or delete without the user's explicit approval of that exact action.
- Use only facts the user gives you or that you can cite. If something is missing, ask instead of guessing.

## Common snags

- Opening the account but never moving the recurring charges. The move is the whole point.
- Using the business card for personal stuff "just this once". That is how it starts.
- Forgetting subscriptions billed to the old card. Audit them during the move.
- Thinking this is only for big businesses. One-person businesses benefit the most.
