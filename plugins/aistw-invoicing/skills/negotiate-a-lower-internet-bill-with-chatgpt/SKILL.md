# Negotiate a Lower Internet Bill with ChatGPT

## When to use

For anyone whose internet promo price ran out, or who has never asked their provider for a better rate.

## Steps

1. Log in to your provider account. Note your monthly price, fees, speed, and whether you're under contract. The broadband label lists the plan's price and fees.
2. Check two or three other providers' sites with your address. Write down the plan, speed, and monthly price for each.
3. Paste your facts into the chat with the prompt below. It drafts a short script and ranks what to ask for.
4. Practice once. Ask the chat to play the retention agent and push back, then adjust your script.
5. Call or message the provider yourself. Ask for the retention or loyalty team, give your competitor price, and ask what they can do.
6. Before you accept, ask for the new price, how long it lasts, and any fees in writing.

## Prompt

Fill the [BRACKETED] parts with the user, then follow this prompt:

```
Help me get a lower internet bill. Don't contact my provider for me.

My provider and plan: [provider, plan name, speed]
What I pay each month, with fees: [amount]
Contract or promo end date: [date or none]
Competitor plans at my address (checked on their sites): [provider, speed, price]
What I want: [lower price / same price with faster speed / remove a fee]
How long I've been a customer: [years]

1. Write a call script under 120 words that's polite and specific.
2. List 3 things to ask for, in order.
3. Then play the provider's retention agent. Push back the way a real agent might, one reply at a time, so I can practice.

Rules: Use only the prices I gave you. If I haven't given a competitor price, tell me to get one first. Don't invent offers.
```

## Guardrails

- Draft first and show the user. Never send, post, pay, book, cancel or delete without the user's explicit approval of that exact action.
- Use only facts the user gives you or that you can cite. If something is missing, ask instead of guessing.

## Common snags

- If no other provider serves your address, you have less to bargain with. Ask about a lower tier or dropping a fee instead.
- A new deal can come with a new contract. Ask before you agree.
- The chat only knows the prices you give it. A stale competitor price weakens the call.
