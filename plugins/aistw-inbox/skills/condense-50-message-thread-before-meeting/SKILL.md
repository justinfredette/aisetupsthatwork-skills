# Condense a 50-Message Thread Before a Meeting

## When to use

A manager or freelancer walking into a meeting about a thread they never had time to read

## Steps

1. Open the thread and copy it, oldest message at the top. Strip nothing; the AI handles the mess.
2. Gmail shortcut: open the thread and click "Summarize this email" at the top. No paste needed, but review it the same way.
3. Paste the thread into ChatGPT with the prompt below. Ask for decisions, positions, open questions, and what the meeting must decide.
4. Read the summary, then verify the facts that matter: names, dates, deadlines, dollar figures. Check them against the original thread.
5. Watch for the word UNCLEAR in the output. Those are the landmines; bring them to the meeting first.
6. Bring the open-questions list into the meeting. Your job is to leave with them answered, not to relitigate the thread.

## Prompt

Fill the [BRACKETED] parts with the user, then follow this prompt:

```
Read this email thread and prepare me for the meeting about it.

Thread:
[PASTE THE THREAD, OLDEST MESSAGE FIRST]

Give me:
1. Decisions already made (quote the message date if you can)
2. Each side's position, in one sentence per side
3. Open questions nobody answered
4. What this meeting needs to decide

Rules: do not guess. If ownership, a deadline, or a decision is unclear, write UNCLEAR and explain why in one line. Then list every name, date, deadline, and commitment I should double-check against the original thread before acting on your summary.
```

## Guardrails

- Draft first and show the user. Never send, post, pay, book, cancel or delete without the user's explicit approval of that exact action.
- Use only facts the user gives you or that you can cite. If something is missing, ask instead of guessing.

## Common snags

- The AI presents guesses as facts. The UNCLEAR rule in the prompt is the fix; do not skip it.
- Pasting a thread with attachments the AI cannot see. If a decision lives in a PDF, summarize that page yourself first.
- Trusting names and dates from the summary alone. One quick scan of the original thread for the three facts that matter.
- Sensitive or confidential threads pasted into a shared AI account. Keep those in Gmail summarize or your company-approved tool.
