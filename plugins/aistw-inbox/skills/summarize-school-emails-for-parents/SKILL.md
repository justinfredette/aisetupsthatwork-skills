# Turn a Week of School Emails Into One Summary

## When to use

A parent who gets 20 school emails a week and is afraid of missing a permission slip

## Steps

1. Create a label or folder called 'School' in your email. Add a filter so emails from the school's domain skip the inbox and land there.
2. Pick a fixed weekly time, 20 minutes, to process the folder. Twice-a-week checkers can use 10 minutes per session instead.
3. Open the folder and skim subject lines. Open anything that looks like a form, a date, or a sign-up. Copy the text of those emails.
4. Paste the emails into Claude or ChatGPT with the prompt below. Include your kids' names and grades so the AI sorts by child.
5. Read the resulting list. Put every date on your calendar right then, before you close the tab.
6. Delete or archive everything in the folder once it is summarized. If it is on your calendar or in the summary, the email is done.

## Prompt

Fill the [BRACKETED] parts with the user, then follow this prompt:

```
Read these school emails and give me one organized summary. My kids: [NAMES AND GRADES, e.g. Connor, 6th grade]. Extract: 1. ACTION NEEDED: forms to fill out, sign-ups, payments, with due dates. 2. DATES: events, early dismissals, no-school days. 3. FYI ONLY: general announcements I can ignore. Sort by child where possible. Keep it short. Emails: [PASTE EMAILS]
```

## Guardrails

- Draft first and show the user. Never send, post, pay, book, cancel or delete without the user's explicit approval of that exact action.
- Use only facts the user gives you or that you can cite. If something is missing, ask instead of guessing.

## Common snags

- The AI misses a deadline buried in a long newsletter. Always double-check the FYI section for anything with a date.
- Emails from different senders about the same kid get mixed up. Label emails by child in your folder, or paste them in separate batches.
- Two parents both act on the same form. Decide which parent handles school forms so you do not double-pay or double-sign.
- The school app has updates that never arrive by email. Glance at the school's app or site during your weekly session too.
