# Write appointment reminder texts for your small business

## When to use

Appointment-based small businesses (salons, clinics, repair shops, consultants) losing money to no-shows.

## Steps

1. List your appointment types and what each confirmation needs: date, time, address, what to bring, late policy.
2. Ask the chatbot to draft three messages per appointment type: instant confirmation, 48-hour reminder, morning-of nudge.
3. Edit for your voice. Short, warm, specific. Cut anything you would not actually say.
4. Add a reply option to each: "Reply YES to confirm or call us to reschedule." Two-way beats one-way.
5. Load the templates into your booking software, phone, or wherever you send reminders from.
6. Get written consent to text customers and include STOP opt-out wording. This is a legal requirement, not a suggestion.

## Prompt

Fill the [BRACKETED] parts with the user, then follow this prompt:

```
Write appointment reminder texts for my business.

BUSINESS: [what you do]
APPOINTMENT TYPES: [e.g. initial consult 60 min, follow-up 30 min]
WHAT CUSTOMERS NEED TO KNOW: [address, parking, what to bring, late policy]
TONE: [warm and professional / casual and friendly]

Write three messages per appointment type:
1. Instant confirmation (right after booking)
2. 48-hour reminder
3. Morning-of nudge

Each under 160 characters where possible. Include a reply prompt (YES to confirm). Add a final line with STOP opt-out wording I can append. Plain words, no hype.
```

## Guardrails

- Draft first and show the user. Never send, post, pay, book, cancel or delete without the user's explicit approval of that exact action.
- Use only facts the user gives you or that you can cite. If something is missing, ask instead of guessing.

## Common snags

- Texting without consent. TCPA rules require prior written consent and a STOP option in every message. Get this right before you send anything.
- Reminder-only systems still lose to no-shows who meant to cancel. The reply-to-reschedule path is what saves the slot.
- AI will happily draft messages that violate carrier rules. You are responsible for compliance, not the chatbot.
