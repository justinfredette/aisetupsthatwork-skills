# Batch a week of social posts in one sitting

## When to use

Small business owners who post in bursts, then go quiet for two weeks because writing captions feels like homework.

## Steps

1. Pick one anchor topic for the week (a job you finished, a question customers keep asking, an offer).
2. Ask the chatbot for 5–7 post angles on that topic, one per day, matched to each platform you use.
3. Draft captions in batches. One prompt per platform, not one prompt for everything.
4. Pair each caption with a visual: a real photo first, a quick Canva graphic second.
5. Read every caption out loud once; cut anything that does not sound like you.
6. Schedule the full week in Buffer or Metricool in one go, then close the apps.
7. Transcribe the video first, then paste the transcript into a chatbot and ask it to list the 5-8 strongest standalone moments with timestamps: a stat, a tip, a story. Show output first; check each moment actually stands alone before you cut it.
8. Reformat each clip for its platform: vertical 9:16 for TikTok, Reels, and Shorts; square or 4:5 for feeds. Caption every clip, since most short-form is watched on mute. One clip, reframed three ways, is three posts.

## Prompt

Fill the [BRACKETED] parts with the user, then follow this prompt:

```
Act as my social media copywriter. My business: [what you do, who you serve, town/region].
This week's anchor topic: [topic].

Write 5 short posts (one for each weekday). For each: a hook in the first line, 2-4 short lines of body, and one soft call to action (call, visit, DM, not "link in bio" unless I have one).
Rules: plain words, no hype, no emojis unless I ask. Sound like a real local business owner, not a marketing agency.
After the posts, list 3 things I should double-check before scheduling (offers, dates, prices I mentioned).
```

## Guardrails

- Draft first and show the user. Never send, post, pay, book, cancel or delete without the user's explicit approval of that exact action.
- Use only facts the user gives you or that you can cite. If something is missing, ask instead of guessing.

## Common snags

- AI captions default to generic, if you would not say it out loud, rewrite it.
- Never let the draft invent an offer, price, or date; verify every claim before scheduling.
- One prompt per platform beats one prompt for "all socials". Batched prompts all sound the same.
- A landscape clip letterboxed into a vertical feed reads as lazy and gets scrolled past. Always reframe to the platform's native aspect ratio.
