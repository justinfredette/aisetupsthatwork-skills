# Never drop a networking contact again

## When to use

Solo operators and freelancers who collect business cards and then never follow up.

## Steps

1. Within 24 hours of the event, write one line per person: name, what you talked about, anything you promised to send.
2. Add each person to your tracker with the date and a status of "to follow up".
3. Paste your notes plus the prompt below into the AI. Get one draft per person.
4. Check each draft. The specific detail from your conversation must be right or the whole thing reads as fake.
5. Send the messages yourself the same day or the next morning.
6. Mark sent in the tracker. Set a reminder for one gentle nudge in 10 to 14 days for the important ones.
7. After the nudge, stop. Respectful silence beats a third chase.

## Prompt

Fill the [BRACKETED] parts with the user, then follow this prompt:

```
Write personal follow-up messages from my networking notes. Each one short enough to send as an email or LinkedIn message. Warm, specific, zero sales pitch.

Event: [EVENT NAME], [DATE]
My business in one line: [WHAT YOU DO]

My notes (one per person):
1. [NAME]: talked about [TOPIC]. I promised to send [THING, or "nothing specific"].
2. [NAME]: talked about [TOPIC]. Mentioned [THEIR CHALLENGE OR INTEREST].
[ADD MORE]

For each person, write:
- A subject line or opening line (3-5 words).
- The message: remind them where we met, reference the specific topic, and include the promised item or one useful thing related to their interest. End with one light next step (a coffee, a call, staying in touch): no hard ask.
- If I promised nothing, do not invent a promise. Just reference the conversation.

Rules: every message must contain the real detail from my notes. If a note is thin, keep the message shorter rather than padding it.
```

## Guardrails

- Draft first and show the user. Never send, post, pay, book, cancel or delete without the user's explicit approval of that exact action.
- Use only facts the user gives you or that you can cite. If something is missing, ask instead of guessing.

## Common snags

- Waiting a week to write up notes. You will forget the details that make follow-ups work. Do it the same night.
- Letting the AI invent conversation details. If your note says "talked about pricing," the draft must not claim you discussed a specific project.
- Following up with everyone the same way. The hot conversation gets a call invite. The brief chat gets a light touch.
