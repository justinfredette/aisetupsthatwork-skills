# Make a Review QR Card Customers Actually Scan

## When to use

Shops, salons, restaurants, and trades with foot traffic or face-to-face handoffs who want reviews without the awkward verbal ask.

## Steps

1. On a computer, open your Google Business Profile, go to Read Reviews, then Get more reviews. Save the QR code image.
2. Decide where the card lives: counter, table tent, receipt insert, or handover folder. Pick one spot to start.
3. Ask AI for three short card copy options: a headline under 8 words, one honest line, and the scan instruction.
4. Lay it out in Canva: your logo, the QR code large enough to scan from a foot away, the copy, nothing else.
5. Print one test copy and scan it with your own phone. Fix anything that fails before you print fifty.
6. Put it out and train staff on one line: "There is a review card on the counter if you have a minute." Then leave it alone.

## Prompt

Fill the [BRACKETED] parts with the user, then follow this prompt:

```
Write copy for a printed Google review QR card for my small business. Short and honest.

Business: [name, type, e.g. bakery, barbershop, plumbing]
Where the card sits: [counter / table / receipt insert / handover folder]

Give me three copy options. Each has:
- A headline under 8 words
- One honest sentence (no hype, no begging)
- A scan instruction under 10 words

Rules:
- Ask for an honest review, never for 5 stars.
- No discounts or prizes mentioned.
- Plainspoken. This card sits next to my register and represents me.
```

## Guardrails

- Draft first and show the user. Never send, post, pay, book, cancel or delete without the user's explicit approval of that exact action.
- Use only facts the user gives you or that you can cite. If something is missing, ask instead of guessing.

## Common snags

- Google only generates the QR code on a desktop browser, not on mobile. Plan for that.
- Tiny QR codes do not scan. Make it at least 1 inch square with quiet space around it.
- Do not require or pressure customers to scan while on your premises. The card invites; staff should not hover.
- Linking to the wrong location is common for multi-location businesses. Scan and verify the profile name.
