# Compare phone and internet plans without the fine-print headache

## When to use

Anyone paying for phone and internet who suspects the promo rate expired months ago and never checked.

## Steps

1. Pull your last bill. Note what you actually pay per month, your data use, and your internet speed.
2. Pick 2 to 3 competing providers and copy their plan details from their sites, including the fine print.
3. Paste everything into the prompt below and ask for a true-cost table over 12 and 24 months.
4. Ask the AI to flag promo pricing that expires, equipment fees, and early termination fees separately.
5. Check the two or three cheapest options against coverage maps for your address before you decide.
6. Call your current provider with the comparison in hand and ask them to match it. Many will.

## Prompt

Fill the [BRACKETED] parts with the user, then follow this prompt:

```
I want to compare phone and internet plans. Here is my situation:
- Current monthly bill: [AMOUNT]
- Phone data I actually use per month: [GB OR "not sure"]
- Internet speed I need: [e.g. streaming, gaming, work video calls]
- People/devices in my household: [NUMBER]
- Budget: [MAX PER MONTH]

Here are the plans I am considering:
[PASTE PLAN 1 DETAILS]
[PASTE PLAN 2 DETAILS]
[PASTE PLAN 3 DETAILS]

Build a table comparing total cost over 12 months and 24 months, including promo pricing, regular pricing after the promo ends, equipment fees, activation fees, and early termination fees. Flag anything in the fine print that changes the real price. Then tell me which plan is cheapest for my situation and why.
```

## Guardrails

- Draft first and show the user. Never send, post, pay, book, cancel or delete without the user's explicit approval of that exact action.
- Use only facts the user gives you or that you can cite. If something is missing, ask instead of guessing.

## Common snags

- The AI can misread promo fine print from a pasted page. Verify the final numbers on the provider site before switching.
- Plan details change often. If your paste is old, the comparison is old.
- The AI may not know about local or regional providers. Add them yourself if they serve your area.
