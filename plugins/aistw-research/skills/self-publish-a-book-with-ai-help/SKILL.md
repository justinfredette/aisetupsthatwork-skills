# Self-Publish a Book With AI Help

## When to use

Aspiring authors with a finished or nearly finished manuscript. AI assists with parts of the process; it does not replace an editor or designer.

## Steps

1. Finish the manuscript first. AI can help brainstorm and organize, but the draft is yours to write.
2. Get a professional edit. At minimum, hire an outside editor; a copyedit for an 80,000-word manuscript takes about two weeks, and good editors book a month or more ahead.
3. Design the cover with a professional designer. The cover is often the number-one marketing tool for the book.
4. Proof the formatted files, then convert to your formats. EPUB gives the best ebook quality.
5. Get ISBNs for each format you publish.
6. Upload to your distribution platforms and check their file specs. On Amazon, books are often live within 24 hours, and preorders can run up to 90 days.
7. Disclose AI-generated content where required. Amazon KDP requires disclosure when AI created the actual content; AI-assisted brainstorming and editing needs no disclosure.
8. Plan marketing separately: the publishing checklist ends at upload.

## Prompt

Fill the [BRACKETED] parts with the user, then follow this prompt:

```
Help me plan my self-publishing project. I will hire an editor and designer; you are the checklist and brainstorming helper.

Book: [title, genre, rough word count]
Formats planned: [ebook, paperback, hardcover, audiobook]
Where I am now: [finished draft, editing, formatting, ready to upload]
AI use so far: [brainstorming only, editing help, none: be honest]

Rules:
- Lay out the remaining steps in order: editing, design, proofing, ISBN, file conversion, upload, marketing.
- For each step, say whether AI can help, a professional is needed, or both.
- Remind me of the KDP rule: disclose AI-generated content (AI created the actual text, images, or translations); no disclosure needed for AI-assisted brainstorming or editing.
- Flag anything I should verify on the platform’s current guidelines page before publishing.
- Do NOT write my book for me or invent publishing facts.

Review the plan before you act on it.
```

## Guardrails

- Draft first and show the user. Never send, post, pay, book, cancel or delete without the user's explicit approval of that exact action.
- Use only facts the user gives you or that you can cite. If something is missing, ask instead of guessing.

## Common snags

- AI cannot replace editing or cover design. Skipping professionals shows in reviews and sales.
- Amazon requires disclosure of AI-generated content, and the definition covers text, images, and translations even if you edited them afterward. Check the current KDP guidelines before publishing.
- Inaccurate metadata like keyword stuffing or false award claims is a policy violation that platforms enforce.
- Marketing is a separate project. The publishing checklist ends at upload; readers do not find the book by themselves.
