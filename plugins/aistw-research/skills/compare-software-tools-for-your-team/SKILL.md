# Compare software tools for your team without the sales calls

## When to use

Small business owners and team leads choosing software who want to skip the sales gauntlet.

## Steps

1. Write down required features, deal-breakers, team size, and your monthly budget. Be strict here.
2. List the 3 to 5 tools you are considering, or ask the AI to suggest candidates for your use case.
3. Ask the AI to build a scored table: features, price at your team size, integrations, ease of use, support quality, and review scores from G2 and Capterra.
4. Verify pricing on the vendors’ own sites. AI pricing data goes stale fast and hides per-seat math.
5. Read a sample of real reviews yourself, especially the 2 and 3 star ones. That is where the truth lives.
6. Trial your top two with real work, not a toy project. Two weeks beats two hours of demo.
7. Decide on fit plus total cost, including onboarding time. The cheapest tool that nobody uses is the most expensive.

## Prompt

Fill the [BRACKETED] parts with the user, then follow this prompt:

```
I need to choose software for my team. Here are my requirements:

Use case: [e.g. project management, invoicing, CRM]
Team size: [NUMBER]
Budget: [MAX PER MONTH, and whether annual billing is OK]
Required features: [LIST]
Deal-breakers: [LIST, e.g. no mobile app, no QuickBooks integration]
Current tools it must work with: [LIST]

Candidates: [LIST 3 TO 5 TOOLS, or ask me to confirm your suggestions first]

Build a comparison table with one row per tool:
- Price at my team size (monthly and annual)
- How it handles each required feature
- Integrations with my current tools
- G2 and Capterra ratings, with what reviewers praise and complain about
- Notable limitations or hidden costs

Then recommend a top two to trial and tell me what to test during the trial. Flag any pricing you are unsure about so I can verify it.
```

## Guardrails

- Draft first and show the user. Never send, post, pay, book, cancel or delete without the user's explicit approval of that exact action.
- Use only facts the user gives you or that you can cite. If something is missing, ask instead of guessing.

## Common snags

- AI pricing is often outdated or missing per-seat tiers. Always confirm on the vendor site.
- Review sites can be gamed. Read the mid-range reviews, not just the average score.
- The AI may not know niche or brand-new tools. Add any you found yourself to the comparison.
