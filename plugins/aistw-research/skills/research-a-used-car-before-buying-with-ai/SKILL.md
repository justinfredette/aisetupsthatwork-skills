# Research a used car before you buy: reliability, price, and scams

## When to use

Anyone shopping for a used car who wants to avoid overpaying or buying someone else’s problem.

## Steps

1. Write down the exact year, make, model, trim, and mileage of the car you are considering.
2. Ask the AI for known reliability issues and expensive repairs for that specific model year.
3. Ask for the fair price range in your area for that mileage and condition.
4. Ask the AI for a pre-purchase inspection checklist tailored to that model’s weak spots.
5. Run a vehicle history report (Carfax or AutoCheck) yourself. The AI cannot do this for you.
6. Book an independent mechanic inspection before you agree to anything. This step is not optional.
7. Use the AI’s price range and the inspection results to negotiate or walk away.

## Prompt

Fill the [BRACKETED] parts with the user, then follow this prompt:

```
I am considering buying this used car:
- Year / make / model / trim: [DETAILS]
- Mileage: [NUMBER]
- Asking price: [AMOUNT]
- Listed by: [dealer or private seller]
- My location: [CITY, STATE]

Tell me:
1. Known reliability issues and expensive repairs for this exact model year.
2. A fair price range for this mileage and condition in my area.
3. Common scams or red flags when buying this type of car from a [dealer/private seller].
4. A pre-purchase inspection checklist focused on this model’s weak spots.
5. Questions I should ask the seller before I visit.

Do not guess at the vehicle history. Flag anything you are unsure about.
```

## Guardrails

- Draft first and show the user. Never send, post, pay, book, cancel or delete without the user's explicit approval of that exact action.
- Use only facts the user gives you or that you can cite. If something is missing, ask instead of guessing.

## Common snags

- The AI can confuse model years or mix up trims. Double-check that its reliability notes match your exact year.
- Price estimates go stale fast. Treat them as a starting point and confirm with current listings.
- The AI cannot pull a vehicle history report or see the actual car. It does not replace the mechanic inspection.
