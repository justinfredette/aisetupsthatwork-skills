# Plan a career change in 90 days

## When to use

Employed people planning a deliberate move into a new field within about three months.

## Steps

1. Days 1-30: pick one target role. Read 20-30 real job descriptions and talk to 3 people doing the work.
2. Map your transferable skills. Split the gaps into required (needed before applying) and nice-to-have.
3. Days 31-60: close the required gaps with one focused course, project, or certification step. Build one visible artifact.
4. Rewrite your resume around the target role, leading with transferable wins.
5. Days 61-90: apply in batches and track what gets responses. Adjust the story, not just the volume.
6. Keep your current income while you research, build proof, and test applications, unless you have a pressing reason to leave sooner.

## Prompt

Fill the [BRACKETED] parts with the user, then follow this prompt:

```
I want to change careers from [CURRENT ROLE] to [TARGET ROLE] in 90 days. My transferable skills are: [LIST]. Draft a week-by-week 90-day plan in three phases (days 1-30 validate, days 31-60 build proof, days 61-90 apply). For each week give one main goal and 2-3 concrete actions. Include a transferable-skills map and a required vs nice-to-have gap list. Show me the plan so I can adjust it.
```

## Guardrails

- Draft first and show the user. Never send, post, pay, book, cancel or delete without the user's explicit approval of that exact action.
- Use only facts the user gives you or that you can cite. If something is missing, ask instead of guessing.

## Common snags

- AI job descriptions go stale. Check posting dates and confirm the role still looks like the listings.
- Applying before your story is clear wastes effort. Clarity comes before applications.
- A 90-day plan is a scaffold, not a promise. Adjust the timeline if the gaps are bigger than expected.
