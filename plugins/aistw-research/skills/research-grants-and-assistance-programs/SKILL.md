# Research grants and assistance programs you actually qualify for

## When to use

Small business owners and nonprofit founders who need funding but drown in generic grant lists.

## Steps

1. Write a one-paragraph profile: business type, employees, revenue, location, years operating, ownership demographics.
2. State exactly what the money is for and the amount. Grant programs fund specific purposes, not vibes.
3. Ask the AI to list matching programs: federal, state, local, and private, with eligibility rules and deadlines.
4. Verify every program on its official page. Grant details change and AI lists go stale.
5. Rank the list by fit and effort. A perfect-fit small grant beats a long-shot big one.
6. Ask the AI to build an application checklist for your top two: documents, narratives, budget format, deadline.
7. Check your local SBDC (Small Business Development Center). Free advisors help with grant applications.

## Prompt

Fill the [BRACKETED] parts with the user, then follow this prompt:

```
I am looking for grants and assistance programs for my business. Here is my profile:

- Business type and industry: [DETAILS]
- Location: [CITY, STATE]
- Employees: [NUMBER]
- Annual revenue: [RANGE]
- Years operating: [NUMBER]
- Ownership: [e.g. woman-owned, veteran-owned, minority-owned, none of these]
- What I need the money for: [SPECIFIC PURPOSE]
- Amount needed: [RANGE]

Please:
1. List grant and assistance programs I plausibly qualify for: federal, state, local, and private. Include the program name, who runs it, and the official link.
2. For each, state the eligibility rules, typical award size, deadline or cycle, and how competitive it is.
3. Flag any program where I clearly do NOT qualify and say why, so I do not waste time.
4. Rank the list by fit times realistic chance of winning.
5. For my top two, list exactly what an application typically requires.

Only include real programs. If you are unsure a program still exists or is currently open, say so and tell me to verify on the official site.
```

## Guardrails

- Draft first and show the user. Never send, post, pay, book, cancel or delete without the user's explicit approval of that exact action.
- Use only facts the user gives you or that you can cite. If something is missing, ask instead of guessing.

## Common snags

- AI grant lists go stale fast. Programs close, pause, or change rules. Verify everything on the official page.
- The AI can mix up similarly named programs or invent plausible-sounding ones. The official link is the test.
- Eligibility is in the details. "Small business" means different employee counts in different programs.
