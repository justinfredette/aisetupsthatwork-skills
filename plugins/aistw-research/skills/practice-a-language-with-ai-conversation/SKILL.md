# Practice a New Language With AI Conversation

## When to use

Language learners who want low-pressure speaking practice between classes or lessons. This is practice, not a course or a teacher.

## Steps

1. Open ChatGPT voice mode and set your preferred voice and target language in Settings.
2. Tell the AI your level and what to do: slow conversational practice, with corrections after you finish each reply.
3. Start with a simple scenario: ordering food, introducing yourself, or small talk.
4. Speak your answers out loud. Do not type them; the speaking is the practice.
5. After each round, ask what to fix and try the corrected version once.
6. Keep sessions short, around 10 to 15 minutes, and repeat the same scenario until it feels easy.
7. Pair this with real instruction. AI practice supplements a course or tutor; it does not replace one.

## Prompt

Fill the [BRACKETED] parts with the user, then follow this prompt:

```
I want to practice speaking [language]. My level is [beginner/intermediate].

Rules:
- Speak to me in [language] at a [slow/natural] pace.
- Give me a simple scenario: [ordering food, introductions, small talk, travel].
- After each of my replies, briefly correct my mistakes, then continue the conversation.
- If I switch to English, nudge me back to [language] gently.
- Keep it encouraging and low pressure.

Review: I will treat your corrections as practice feedback, not a teacher’s verdict.
```

## Guardrails

- Draft first and show the user. Never send, post, pay, book, cancel or delete without the user's explicit approval of that exact action.
- Use only facts the user gives you or that you can cite. If something is missing, ask instead of guessing.

## Common snags

- AI corrections may be wrong or incomplete. Treat them as practice feedback, not a teacher’s verdict.
- Voice mode has usage limits that vary by plan. Check your plan before a long session.
- No source here establishes how effective AI practice is for learning. Pair it with real instruction.
- The AI may drift into English or go too fast. Tell it to slow down or switch back.
