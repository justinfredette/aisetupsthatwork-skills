# Outline a novel with AI and keep continuity

## When to use

Fiction writers starting a novel who want a structured outline and a way to stop characters changing eye color in chapter twelve.

## Steps

1. Write your premise down first: who wants what, what stands in the way, what changes. Two or three sentences is enough.
2. Ask the AI to expand the premise into a three-act outline using a beat sheet. Reedsy describes the Save the Cat beat sheet as 15 beats built on the three-act structure, designed to carry writers through the middle of Act 2.
3. Build the story bible next: for each character, one paragraph on who they are, what they want, and one detail that makes them specific. Add the world rules that cannot bend.
4. Feed the story bible back into every outline session. Sudowrite documents its Story Bible as the "source of truth" the writer and the AI refer back to, and notes that generation only uses characters and worldbuilding elements you explicitly mention.
5. Go beat by beat, not chapter by chapter. Ask for three options per beat, pick one, and lock it into the bible before moving on.
6. When the outline is done, ask the AI to audit it: which beats contradict the story bible, which characters disappear for too long, where the stakes go flat.
7. Review the full outline yourself and rewrite anything that sounds like a template. Reedsy advises that outline models are scaffolding, not paint-by-numbers, and originality comes from character-specific choices.

## Prompt

Fill the [BRACKETED] parts with the user, then follow this prompt:

```
Help me outline a novel and keep the facts consistent.

Premise: [e.g. "A retired lighthouse keeper discovers the light never went out on its own: someone has been tending it for 40 years"]
Genre and tone: [e.g. "literary mystery, quiet and melancholic"]
Target length: [e.g. "80,000 words"]

First, expand this into a three-act outline using a 15-beat structure. Give me each beat as: beat name, one-sentence description, and the chapter range it covers.

Then create my story bible:
- Main characters: name, one paragraph each (who they are, what they want, one specific detail)
- World rules: the 3-5 facts about this world that can never be contradicted
- Open threads: questions the outline must answer by the end

Rules:
- Flag any beat that contradicts the story bible or the premise.
- Do not invent new main characters without asking me first.
- Keep everything in plain prose, no purple adjectives.

Review the outline before using it. The story bible is yours to keep and feed back into every future session.
```

## Guardrails

- Draft first and show the user. Never send, post, pay, book, cancel or delete without the user's explicit approval of that exact action.
- Use only facts the user gives you or that you can cite. If something is missing, ask instead of guessing.

## Common snags

- AI invents details you never approved: new siblings, changed backstories, a town that moves counties. The audit step exists to catch these.
- The outline reads like every other book in the genre. That is the template showing through; rewrite beats with character-specific choices.
- Continuity only works if the story bible is complete. Anything you keep in your head and never write down will get contradicted.
