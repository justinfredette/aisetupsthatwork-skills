# Resignation letter and exit conversation

## When to use

Employees leaving a job who want the letter and the meeting handled cleanly.

## Steps

1. Check your contract for the notice period. Some require 30, 60, or 90 days, and shorting it can breach the contract.
2. Decide your last day. Two weeks is the standard professional courtesy in the US, where most jobs are at-will.
3. Use the prompt below to draft the letter: a few short paragraphs, no grievances, no detail about the next move.
4. Tell your manager first in a private one-on-one meeting (video if remote). Hand over the letter there.
5. Send a copy to HR after the manager conversation to trigger formal offboarding.
6. Offer two weeks of transition help. Short, useful handoffs are remembered.

## Prompt

Fill the [BRACKETED] parts with the user, then follow this prompt:

```
I am resigning from my role as [TITLE] at [COMPANY]. My last day will be [DATE], which meets my [NOTICE PERIOD] notice requirement. Draft a resignation letter of 3-4 short paragraphs: a direct statement of resignation, my title and last day, one line of thanks, and an offer to help with the transition. No explanation of why I am leaving, no mention of the next employer, no grievances. Professional and warm. Show me the draft.
```

## Guardrails

- Draft first and show the user. Never send, post, pay, book, cancel or delete without the user's explicit approval of that exact action.
- Use only facts the user gives you or that you can cite. If something is missing, ask instead of guessing.

## Common snags

- AI does not know your contract. Confirm the notice period yourself before naming a last day.
- Do not use the letter to air grievances or criticize colleagues. It becomes part of your file.
- Do not name your next employer in the letter. It is not required and adds nothing.
