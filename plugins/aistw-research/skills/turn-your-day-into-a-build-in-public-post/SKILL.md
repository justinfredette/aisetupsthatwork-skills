# Turn your day into a build-in-public post

## When to use

Founders and side-hustlers building an app or business who know they should post daily but never know what to say.

## Steps

1. At the end of the day, write 5 rough lines: what you shipped, what broke, what you learned, one number, what is next.
2. Paste them into the prompt below with 3 of your past posts so it can match your voice.
3. Pick the version that leads with the most interesting true thing. Failures often beat wins.
4. Edit until it sounds like you, and check every number.
5. Post it yourself. One founder who built an app in public advises sharing successes and failures so people get to know the person behind it.
6. Save the notes; a month of them is your launch story.

## Prompt

Fill the [BRACKETED] parts with the user, then follow this prompt:

```
Turn today's notes into one build-in-public post. Use only my facts.

Today's notes: [5 ROUGH LINES]
3 of my past posts, for voice: [PASTE]

Rules: under 280 characters for X (or under 120 words for LinkedIn), lead with the most interesting true thing, include the one number if I gave one, plain words, no hashtags, no hype words, no claims I did not make. Give 3 versions: one leading with a win, one with a failure, one with a lesson.
```

## Guardrails

- Draft first and show the user. Never send, post, pay, book, cancel or delete without the user's explicit approval of that exact action.
- Use only facts the user gives you or that you can cite. If something is missing, ask instead of guessing.

## Common snags

- Letting AI invent excitement the notes do not support.
- Posting only wins, which reads like an ad.
- Skipping days when nothing went right; those make good posts.
