# How to use AI for business plan research: locations, costs and funding

## When to use

People who need answers on where a business should be, what it will cost, who might fund it, and how people will find it, before they bet on the idea.

## Steps

1. Location bot: compares 3 to 5 places you're considering, with risks, pros, and cons for each.
2. Money bot: rough startup and monthly cost estimates, plus grants, loans, and other funding to look into.
3. Marketing bot: who the first customers are, where they hang out, and a simple launch plan.
4. Review step: checks your own CV or background against the plan and points out gaps.

## Prompt

Fill the [BRACKETED] parts with the user, then follow this prompt:

```
I'm researching a business plan for [BUSINESS IDEA] in [REGION].
1. Compare these locations: [PLACE 1], [PLACE 2], [PLACE 3]. For each: risks, pros, cons, rough rent or space cost, and who nearby would buy.
2. Estimate startup costs and monthly running costs as ranges. Show where each number came from.
3. List funding options to check (grants, loans, small-business programs) with links to the official pages.
4. Draft a simple marketing plan for the first 90 days.
5. Read my background below and list the gaps I'd need to fill.
Mark every number as an estimate and every claim you couldn't source.
My background: [PASTE CV OR SUMMARY]
```

## Guardrails

- Draft first and show the user. Never send, post, pay, book, cancel or delete without the user's explicit approval of that exact action.
- Use only facts the user gives you or that you can cite. If something is missing, ask instead of guessing.

## Common snags

- Cost and funding numbers from AI are starting points. Rents, grant rules, and deadlines change, so confirm each one with the landlord, lender, or program before it goes into a real plan.
