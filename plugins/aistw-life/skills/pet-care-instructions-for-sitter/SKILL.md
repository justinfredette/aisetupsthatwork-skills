# Pet Care Instructions for Your Sitter (One Sheet, No Panic Texts)

## When to use

Pet owners leaving town who currently explain everything verbally and then get “quick question” texts all weekend. Works for dogs, cats, and multi-pet homes.

## Steps

1. Write down the full routine for one normal day: feeding times and portions, walks, litter, meds, bedtime.
2. List the quirks: triggers, fears, escape habits, food stealing, other-pet rules.
3. Gather contacts: your cell, backup person, vet, 24-hour emergency vet.
4. Paste it all into the prompt and ask for a one-page sheet with emergencies first.
5. Print two copies: one for the sitter, one on the fridge.
6. Walk through it together before you leave. Paper does not replace the tour.
7. Update the sheet before every trip. Routines change.

## Prompt

Fill the [BRACKETED] parts with the user, then follow this prompt:

```
Write me a one-page pet sitter instruction sheet.

Pets: [name, species, breed, age, e.g. "Moose, lab mix, 4"]
Feeding: [food brand, portion, times, e.g. "2 cups Taste of the Wild, 7 AM and 5 PM"]
Water: [refill routine]
Walks / litter: [times and duration, e.g. "20-min walk 7:30 AM and 6 PM, fenced yard otherwise"]
Medications: [name, dose, time, how to give: or "none"]
Quirks: [e.g. "bolts out the front door, scared of thunder, steals food off counters"]
Other pets: [how they interact, feeding separation]

Contacts:
- My cell: [number]
- Backup person: [name, number]
- Vet: [name, number]
- Emergency vet: [name, number]

House: [key/code location, alarm code, wifi password]

Trip dates: [start to end]

Rules:
- Emergency contacts and vet info at the very top, bold.
- Daily schedule as a timeline with times.
- Medications with exact dose, time, and method. No vagueness.
- One "if this happens" section: missed dose, escape, vomiting, power outage.
- Keep it to one printable page. Cut fluff.
- Draft only. I verify every phone number before printing.
```

## Guardrails

- Draft first and show the user. Never send, post, pay, book, cancel or delete without the user's explicit approval of that exact action.
- Use only facts the user gives you or that you can cite. If something is missing, ask instead of guessing.

## Common snags

- Vague instructions like “feed twice” cause the exact panic texts you are trying to avoid. Portions, times, and locations must be specific.
- Forgetting house details: alarm codes, key location, and wifi. The sitter needs the house sheet too, not just the pet sheet.
- An outdated sheet is worse than none. Update meds, food, and contacts before every single trip.
