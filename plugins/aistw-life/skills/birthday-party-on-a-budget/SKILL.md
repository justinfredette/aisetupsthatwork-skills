# Plan a Kid’s Birthday Party on a Budget

## When to use

Parents planning a kid’s birthday who want it fun without the $500 price tag. Works for home, park, or backyard parties.

## Steps

1. Set the budget ceiling before anything else. Every decision flows from this number.
2. Decide the venue: home and public parks are free. That is the biggest lever.
3. List the guest count. Every guest you cut saves roughly $10 in food, favors, and cake.
4. Paste budget, age, guests, and venue into the prompt.
5. Get the line-item plan: food, cake, decor, games, favors.
6. Time the party between meals (2 to 4 PM). Snacks replace a full meal and cut food costs sharply.
7. Buy decor at the dollar store. Kids do not audit your tableware.

## Prompt

Fill the [BRACKETED] parts with the user, then follow this prompt:

```
Plan my kid's birthday party inside this budget.

Budget ceiling: [total dollars, e.g. "$150"]
Kid's age: [e.g. "6"]
Guest count: [number of kids]
Venue: [home / backyard / public park]
Date and time: [e.g. "Saturday 2-4 PM"]
Theme (optional): [e.g. "dinosaurs" or "none, keep it simple"]

Rules:
- Line-item budget: food, cake, decorations, games/entertainment, favors. Every line needs a dollar amount that sums to my ceiling.
- Food: snacks and finger foods, not a full meal. Suggest a DIY station (nacho bar, mini pizzas, sundae bar).
- Cake: homemade or grocery store sheet cake, not a bakery custom cake, unless budget allows.
- Entertainment: free or cheap games matched to the age. No hired entertainers unless I ask.
- Favors: cheap take-home crafts or skip them. No plastic trinket bags.
- Flag the three most common budget traps for this kind of party.
- Draft only. I buy and book everything myself.
```

## Guardrails

- Draft first and show the user. Never send, post, pay, book, cancel or delete without the user's explicit approval of that exact action.
- Use only facts the user gives you or that you can cite. If something is missing, ask instead of guessing.

## Common snags

- The AI may plan a Pinterest party that quietly exceeds your ceiling. Make it show the math and cut until it fits.
- Guest count creep is the real budget killer. Lock the list before you plan anything else.
- Pinterest-style decor suggestions can assume supplies you do not own. Ask for dollar-store-only decor.
