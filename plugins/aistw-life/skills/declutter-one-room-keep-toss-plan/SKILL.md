# Declutter One Room With a Keep / Toss / Donate Plan

## When to use

Anyone with one room they avoid (spare bedroom, office, closet). Not for whole-house overhauls. One room, one afternoon.

## Steps

1. Choose one room. Not two. The whole point is finishing.
2. Describe the room to the AI: size, what lives there, and the worst zones.
3. Ask for a zone order (start where the mess bothers you most) and decision rules for common items.
4. Set a timer for 30 minutes. Sort fast: keep, toss, donate, relocate.
5. Use the AI’s decision shortcuts for stuck items: “haven’t used it in a year” goes to donate.
6. When the timer ends, take toss bags out and put donate bags in your car immediately.
7. Repeat for the next zone tomorrow. Do not try to finish the room in one day.

## Prompt

Fill the [BRACKETED] parts with the user, then follow this prompt:

```
Help me declutter one room with a clear plan.

Room: [e.g. "spare bedroom, 10x12, used as storage"]
Worst zones: [e.g. "closet full of old clothes, desk covered in papers, boxes from our move 2 years ago"]
Time I have: [e.g. "two 30-minute sessions this weekend"]
Sentimental traps: [e.g. "kids' old school papers, gifts I never liked"]

Rules:
- Break the room into zones in the order I should tackle them.
- For each zone, give me 3 decision rules (e.g. "clothes unworn in 12 months go to donate").
- Include a "stuck item" script: 3 questions to ask when I cannot decide.
- Sentimental items get a photo-and-let-go option, not a guilt trip.
- End with a "finish line" checklist: bags out, donate in car, surfaces clear.
- You plan and decide with me. I do the physical sorting.
```

## Guardrails

- Draft first and show the user. Never send, post, pay, book, cancel or delete without the user's explicit approval of that exact action.
- Use only facts the user gives you or that you can cite. If something is missing, ask instead of guessing.

## Common snags

- The AI may suggest organizing systems (bins, labels) before you finish sorting. Sort first, organize later.
- “Maybe” piles grow fast. Cap it: one small box, and anything still in it after a week goes to donate.
- Paper is the slowest category. Ask for a separate paper-only session plan instead of mixing it in.
