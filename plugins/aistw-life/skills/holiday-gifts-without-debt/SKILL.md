# Plan Holiday Gifts Without the January Debt Hangover

## When to use

Anyone who has ever started January with holiday debt. Especially useful for big families where the gift list has outgrown the budget.

## Steps

1. Decide the total holiday number first: gifts, food, travel, decor, everything.
2. List every person you buy for. Be honest about the full list.
3. Assign a per-person limit. The total must fit inside the holiday number.
4. Paste it into the prompt and get gift ideas at each price point.
5. For big families, propose a gift exchange (draw names) instead of buying for everyone.
6. Track every purchase as you go. Move unspent money between people or bank it.
7. Use cash or debit for gift runs if credit cards tempt you to drift.

## Prompt

Fill the [BRACKETED] parts with the user, then follow this prompt:

```
Build me a holiday gift plan that keeps me out of debt.

Total holiday budget: [all-in number, e.g. "$800"]
Gift list with per-person limits:
[e.g. "Mom $75, Dad $75, kids $100 each, sister $40, coworkers: skip"]
People I need ideas for: [names and a line about each, e.g. "Dad, 68, likes grilling"]
Hard nos: [e.g. "no gift cards over $25, nothing that needs assembly"]
Shopping style: [cash/debit only, or "credit but paid off monthly"]

Rules:
- Every person gets 2 to 3 gift ideas at or under their limit.
- Suggest one cheaper alternative for each expensive idea.
- Include homemade and experience gifts where they fit.
- Give me a tracking table: person, limit, gift chosen, actual spent, remaining.
- If my per-person limits exceed my total budget, say so and make me cut.
- Flag buy-now-pay-later traps: show the true total cost if I mention them.
- Draft only. I buy everything myself.
```

## Guardrails

- Draft first and show the user. Never send, post, pay, book, cancel or delete without the user's explicit approval of that exact action.
- Use only facts the user gives you or that you can cite. If something is missing, ask instead of guessing.

## Common snags

- The AI may suggest gifts above your per-person limits. Make it show prices and cut until it fits.
- Forgetting the non-gift costs: food, travel, decor, and shipping eat budgets silently. Include them in the total.
- Buy-now-pay-later feels free and is not. Ask the AI to total the real cost before you click.
