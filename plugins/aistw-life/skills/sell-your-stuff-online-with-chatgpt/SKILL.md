# Sell Your Stuff Online with ChatGPT

## When to use

For anyone with a closet, garage, or basement full of things they keep meaning to sell, but who stalls on writing listings and guessing prices.

## Steps

1. Photograph each item in good light from a few angles. Include the brand label or model number and any scratches or wear.
2. Upload the photos with the prompt below. Do 3 to 5 items per chat so details don't get mixed up.
3. Check what the chat identified. Fix any wrong brand, model, or size before you use the listing.
4. Look at the price range and the similar listings it links. Sold prices tell you more than asking prices, so check sold listings on the site yourself when you can.
5. Post each listing yourself and paste in the title and description.
6. When buyers message you, ask the chat for a short, polite reply if you need one. Meet in a public place, and don't accept a check for more than your price.

## Prompt

Fill the [BRACKETED] parts with the user, then follow this prompt:

```
Help me write listings to sell some things I own. Don't post anything for me.

Where I'm selling: [Facebook Marketplace, eBay, Craigslist, etc.]
My area: [city or region]
Attached: photos of each item.

For each item:
1. Tell me what you think it is (brand, model, size) and how sure you are. Ask me if you can't tell.
2. Write a listing title under 80 characters with the words a buyer would search.
3. Write a short, honest description: what it is, condition, any wear or damage visible in the photos, and pickup or shipping.
4. Search the web for similar items for sale or recently sold and suggest a price range. Link what you based it on.

Rules: Don't invent features, model numbers, or condition details. Mention any damage you can see. If you can't find comparable prices, say so instead of guessing.
```

## Guardrails

- Draft first and show the user. Never send, post, pay, book, cancel or delete without the user's explicit approval of that exact action.
- Use only facts the user gives you or that you can cite. If something is missing, ask instead of guessing.

## Common snags

- The chat can misidentify a brand or model from a photo. Check it against the label before you post.
- Price suggestions come from listings web search can find. Some selling sites limit what search can see, so prices can be off. Check sold listings on the site yourself.
- The chat can't post listings or message buyers for you, so posting and replying are still on you.
