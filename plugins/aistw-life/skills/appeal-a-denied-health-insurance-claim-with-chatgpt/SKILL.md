# Appeal a Denied Health Insurance Claim with ChatGPT

## When to use

For anyone whose health plan refused to pay for a visit, test, or treatment and who isn't sure what to write back.

## Steps

1. Find the date on the denial notice. HealthCare.gov says you have 180 days from that notice to file an internal appeal. Put the deadline on your calendar.
2. Black out your member ID and Social Security number. Upload the denial letter and the plan pages it mentions with the prompt below.
3. Read the plain-English summary of why the claim was denied, and check it against the letter yourself.
4. Ask your doctor for a short letter that answers the denial reason, and add it to the chat.
5. Have the chat draft the appeal letter and a checklist of what to attach. Use your insurer's form if it has one.
6. Send the appeal yourself and keep copies. Log every phone call with the date, time, and name of the person you spoke to.
7. If the plan says no again, its final letter has to explain how to ask for an external review. You have 4 months from that notice to file.

## Prompt

Fill the [BRACKETED] parts with the user, then follow this prompt:

```
Help me appeal a denied health insurance claim. Don't send anything or contact anyone for me.

Attached: the denial letter, the plan pages it cites, and my doctor's note if I have one. I've blacked out my ID numbers.

1. In plain English, tell me the exact reason for the denial and quote the line from the letter.
2. Pull out any deadline, claim number, and appeal address or form the letter mentions.
3. Draft an internal appeal letter under one page: my name, claim number, the service and date, why it should be covered, and which attached documents back that up. Use only facts from my documents.
4. List what to attach and what I still need to get.

Rules: Don't invent medical facts, policy language, or laws. If the denial reason is unclear, tell me what to ask the insurer.
```

## Guardrails

- Draft first and show the user. Never send, post, pay, book, cancel or delete without the user's explicit approval of that exact action.
- Use only facts the user gives you or that you can cite. If something is missing, ask instead of guessing.

## Common snags

- The chat can misread plan language. Check every quote against the document before you send.
- Use the deadlines printed in your own denial letter. Your plan papers are the final word for your case.
- An appeal without your doctor's reasoning may leave a medical-necessity denial unanswered.
