# Weekly Home Reset Checklist (A Plan You Will Actually Follow)

## When to use

Anyone whose cleaning happens in panicked bursts before guests arrive. Works for apartments and houses, solo or with family pitching in.

## Steps

1. Walk through your home and list every room plus the one task each room needs most.
2. Decide your daily time budget. Twenty minutes a day beats a four-hour Saturday you will skip.
3. Tell the AI your rooms, your minutes, and who helps: kids, partner, roommate, or just you.
4. Ask for a Monday-to-Sunday plan with one focus area per day and a 10-minute nightly reset.
5. Add a skip list: jobs like baseboards and windows that can wait two weeks without consequences.
6. Run the plan for one week, then tell the AI what felt too heavy and ask it to rebalance.

## Prompt

Fill the [BRACKETED] parts with the user, then follow this prompt:

```
Build me a weekly home reset plan I can actually stick to.

My home: [apartment/house, number of bedrooms and bathrooms, e.g. "2-bed apartment, 1 bath"]
People: [who lives here and ages, e.g. "me, partner, kids 6 and 9"]
Pets: [none / dog / cat: note shedding]
Time I have: [minutes per weekday, e.g. "20 minutes Mon-Fri, 45 on Saturday"]
Helpers: [who does chores and what they already handle]

Rules:
- One focus area per day, nothing over my time budget.
- Include a 10-minute nightly reset (dishes, counters, toy sweep, trash check).
- Add a "skip list" of tasks that can safely wait 2+ weeks.
- Keep tasks concrete: "wipe bathroom sink and mirror," not "clean bathroom."
- Output as a Monday-to-Sunday checklist I can print or copy to my notes app.
- Do not schedule anything that needs supplies I did not list.
```

## Guardrails

- Draft first and show the user. Never send, post, pay, book, cancel or delete without the user's explicit approval of that exact action.
- Use only facts the user gives you or that you can cite. If something is missing, ask instead of guessing.

## Common snags

- The AI may pack too much into each day. Tell it your real minutes and ask it to cut, not cram.
- Vague tasks like “tidy living room” get ignored. Ask for specific actions with a clear done state.
- If nobody enforces the plan, it dies by week two. Assign one owner per day, even if that owner is you.
