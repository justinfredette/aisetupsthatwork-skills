# Laid off: first-week plan

## When to use

People who were just laid off and need to know what to do first, in order.

## Steps

1. Read your separation notice. Note your last day, severance terms, and when health coverage ends.
2. File for unemployment insurance with your state as soon as you can. You will need your last job start and end dates and your former employer address.
3. Protect health coverage: check COBRA eligibility, a spouse plan special enrollment (usually requested within 30 days of losing coverage), or Marketplace options.
4. List every bill and its due date. Call lenders about hardship options before you miss a payment.
5. Use your state resource finder or dial 211 for free local referrals to food, cash, and counseling help.
6. Only then start the job search. Week one is about the safety net; the resume can wait a few days.

## Prompt

Fill the [BRACKETED] parts with the user, then follow this prompt:

```
I was laid off. My last day is [DATE], severance is [TERMS], and health coverage ends [DATE]. My state is [STATE]. Draft a week-one checklist in priority order covering: unemployment filing, health coverage options to compare, bills to triage, and documents to gather. Do not ask for or store my SSN, account numbers, or other identifying details. Flag anything I must confirm with my state unemployment site or a professional. Show me the checklist.
```

## Guardrails

- Draft first and show the user. Never send, post, pay, book, cancel or delete without the user's explicit approval of that exact action.
- Use only facts the user gives you or that you can cite. If something is missing, ask instead of guessing.

## Common snags

- AI does not know your state rules. Confirm filing steps, deadlines, and benefit amounts on your state site.
- Never paste your SSN, bank details, or full medical records into a chatbot.
- Severance and benefits letters have real deadlines. Read them before the week slips away.
