# How to Use ChatGPT to Write a Letter of Recommendation

## When to use

Managers asked to write the fifth reference this month, or teachers who want a structured draft they still personalize.

## Steps

1. Confirm you can recommend them honestly, if not, decline instead of prompting.
2. Paste facts only: how you know them, dates, role, strengths backed by examples, areas of growth if appropriate.
3. Paste the target (grad school, job title, scholarship) and length limit.
4. Ask for formal letter format with opening, two example paragraphs, closing, and signature block with your title.
5. Forbid invented metrics, awards, or superlatives you did not supply.
6. You export to PDF or paste into email. You send; no agent mail access.

## Prompt

Fill the [BRACKETED] parts with the user, then follow this prompt:

```
You help me draft a letter of recommendation. Do not send email or submit forms for me.

Candidate name: [name]
My relationship: [manager / professor / client]
Dates known: [range]
Role / context: [title, class, project]

Facts I can stand behind (only use these: do not invent):
[paste bullets: projects, skills, outcomes, character with examples]

They are applying to: [program / job / scholarship]
Length limit: [e.g. one page]
Tone: [warm professional / formal academic]

Rules:
- If I did not provide a fact, do not add grades, rankings, or awards.
- Include one specific story from my bullets; no generic filler.
- Note where I should personalize the opening sentence in [brackets].
- Draft only: I approve before anything is sent, spent, or uploaded.
```

## Guardrails

- Draft first and show the user. Never send, post, pay, book, cancel or delete without the user's explicit approval of that exact action.
- Use only facts the user gives you or that you can cite. If something is missing, ask instead of guessing.

## Common snags

- Models upgrade weak candidates into “top 1%”. Delete hype you cannot testify to.
- Academic integrity policies may restrict AI help. Check your school or employer rules.
- Uploading student records to cloud chat can be a FERPA/privacy issue. Use redacted bullets.
