# Break an Overwhelming Task Into Small Steps With AI

## When to use

Anyone who stalls out staring at a big task, including people with ADHD who find starting the hardest part. This is a productivity helper, not treatment.

## Steps

1. Write the task in one sentence, exactly as it sits in your head. Vague is fine.
2. Paste it into the chat with the prompt below and ask for steps no longer than 10 minutes each.
3. Read the list and delete or merge anything that does not fit your situation.
4. Pick the single first step and ignore the rest for now. One step at a time is the whole method.
5. Set a timer for 10 minutes and do only that step.
6. When the timer ends, check the step off and pick the next one. Stopping is also fine.
7. If you keep stalling on the same step, ask the AI to split that one step into even smaller pieces.

## Prompt

Fill the [BRACKETED] parts with the user, then follow this prompt:

```
Break this task into the smallest possible steps. Each step must take 10 minutes or less for one person.

Task: [describe the task in one sentence]

Rules:
- List steps in the order I would do them.
- Number each step. One action per step.
- Mark the very first step clearly so I know where to start.
- Do not add tools, apps, or purchases I did not mention.
- If a step still looks big, split it further.

Review the list before you use it: delete or merge anything that does not fit, then do only step one.
```

## Guardrails

- Draft first and show the user. Never send, post, pay, book, cancel or delete without the user's explicit approval of that exact action.
- Use only facts the user gives you or that you can cite. If something is missing, ask instead of guessing.

## Common snags

- AI is a list-making helper, not treatment for ADHD. If task paralysis keeps happening, talk to a clinician or coach.
- The AI may assume tools, energy, or time you do not have. Edit the list to match your real situation.
- Steps that depend on other people need their own follow-up. AI cannot chase them for you.
- Do not paste sensitive work details into a chat. Describe the task, not the confidential parts.
