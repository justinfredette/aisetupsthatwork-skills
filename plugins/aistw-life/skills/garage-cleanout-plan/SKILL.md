# Garage Cleanout Plan (One Weekend, Four Piles)

## When to use

Anyone whose garage has not fit a car in years. Best done on a dry weekend with one helper and a dump or donation run planned in advance.

## Steps

1. Pick the weekend and check the forecast. You need a dry driveway for staging.
2. Describe the garage to the AI: size, what is in it, and your end goal.
3. Get the plan: Friday pull-out, Saturday sort and load, Sunday put-back. Or one big day if the garage is small.
4. Pull everything onto the driveway. Seeing it all at once is the point.
5. Sort into four zones: keep, donate, sell, toss. Decide once; no second-guessing piles.
6. Load the toss pile for the dump and get donations out of the house within 48 hours.
7. Sweep, then put back only what earns its space, in zones: tools, sports, seasonal, car.
8. Photograph the finished layout. Next cleanout starts from this picture.

## Prompt

Fill the [BRACKETED] parts with the user, then follow this prompt:

```
Plan my garage cleanout weekend.

Garage: [e.g. "2-car garage, packed floor to ceiling on one side"]
What's in there: [e.g. "tools, kids' bikes, holiday decor, boxes from our 2022 move, old paint cans"]
Goal: [e.g. "fit one car" / "find my tools" / "full reset"]
Help: [e.g. "me plus my partner Saturday" / "solo"]
Disposal: [e.g. "town dump open Saturdays, Goodwill 10 min away"]
Hazardous stuff: [e.g. "old paint, motor oil: need proper disposal"]

Rules:
- Day-by-day plan: pull-out, sort, load, put-back. Adjust to my help and garage size.
- Four sorting zones: keep, donate, sell, toss. Give me decision rules for each.
- Tell me what goes to hazardous waste, not the regular dump.
- Put-back layout with zones: tools, sports, seasonal, car space. Vertical storage first.
- Include a "don't bring it back inside" rule for the keep pile.
- End with a maintenance habit so it doesn't refill in a year.
- You plan. I lift, sort, and haul.
```

## Guardrails

- Draft first and show the user. Never send, post, pay, book, cancel or delete without the user's explicit approval of that exact action.
- Use only facts the user gives you or that you can cite. If something is missing, ask instead of guessing.

## Common snags

- Sorting without a dump plan. The toss pile sits for a month and migrates back inside.
- “Maybe” items with no decision rule. If you have not used it in two years, it is donate or toss.
- Putting everything back without zones. Unzoned storage refills randomly within a year.
